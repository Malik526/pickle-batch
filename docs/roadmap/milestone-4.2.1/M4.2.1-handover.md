## Design (implemented)

- **No schema change.** A derivative's identity is its private storage key:
  `users/<user_id>/videos/<video_id>/derived/instagram/reel-<POLICY_VERSION>-<file_hash[:32]>.mp4`.
  - Exists → reuse, with no download and no encode.
  - A changed `videos.file_hash` or a bumped `normalization.POLICY_VERSION` → new key, so the
    derivative regenerates.
  - Only one worker holds a claimed post, and the upload is an upsert of a verified file, so
    concurrent attempts can't conflict.
- **Decision on display dimensions** (rotation-aware), via the new `media/stream_probe.py`.
- **Hard limits stay user-facing failures:** duration 3 s–15 min (`check_duration`), plus caption
  limits. No trimming or padding.
- **Normalizable** (`media_requirements.compatibility_problems`): container not MOV/MP4; codec
  not H.264/HEVC; pixel format not 8-bit 4:2:0 or HDR; display width > 1920; fps outside 23–60;
  video bitrate > 25 Mbps; audio not AAC ≤48 kHz ≤2 ch; size > 300 MB. A compatible original is
  published untouched.
- **Output policy** (`normalization.py`):
  - one ffmpeg pass: libx264 High, CRF 20, `veryfast`, yuv420p, maxrate 10 Mbps (lowered to
    keep long videos ≤90% of 300 MB), GOP 2 s, closed;
  - scaled so the **longer side ≤1920** (portrait 4K → 1080×1920, landscape → 1920×1080),
    even sides, never upscaled or cropped;
  - fps kept when 23–60, >60 → 60, <23 → 30;
  - audio copied if compliant, else AAC 128k 48 kHz stereo;
  - HDR (HLG/PQ) tone-mapped with zscale;
  - `-map_metadata -1` (drops GPS etc.); `+faststart` MP4; rotation baked into the pixels (ffmpeg
    autorotate);
  - output re-probed and required compatible and the same length (±max(0.5 s, 1%)).
- **Heartbeat:** while ffmpeg runs, the claimed post's `updated_at` is refreshed about every
  60 s, so crash recovery on another replica never sees a long encode as a stale claim (that
  could otherwise lead to a duplicate container). Timeout:
  `INSTAGRAM_NORMALIZATION_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` (default 3600) → `INSTAGRAM_MEDIA_PREPARATION_TIMEOUT`.

## Limitations to document

- No cropping, reframing, trimming, padding, captions burned in, or TikTok/YouTube normalization.
- Encoding runs inline in the worker loop: a long 4K encode delays other users' posts that cycle.
  It's structured so `prepare_publishable_media` can move to a processing worker later.
- HDR tone-mapping needs ffmpeg with zscale. Bookworm's ffmpeg 5.1 (Docker) and local 4.4 both
  have it. HDR output has not been verified against a real iPhone HDR file.
- The derivative is trusted on reuse (it was verified before upload); it isn't re-probed.
